## Quick notes for cultural adaptation (already applied in suggestions above)

**Spanish (LatAm):**
- Use "ustedes" style, warmer and more direct.
- Emphasize "accesibilidad para PYMES familiares".
- Mention expansion to LATAM where natural.

**Arabic / Shariah-aware:**
- Emphasize asset-backed, real ownership, no riba (trade margin instead of interest).
- "Facilidades de financiamiento comercial" and "Programas de proveedor de rendimientos" sound more appropriate than pure "equity" language in some markets.
- Keep language formal and professional.
